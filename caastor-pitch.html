01  ·  How We're Different

Three layers.
Most platforms
have one.

Other creative platforms give you execution. We give you strategy that sets up the work, execution that routes each job to the right model, and human craft that closes what matters most.

01 / 03
Strategic

Your brief gets sharper before work starts.

Every engagement starts with a strategic read. Our AI operator loads your brand intelligence, reviews the request, and surfaces a stronger version of the brief — with the reasoning visible so you can accept, edit, or redirect.

No more back-and-forth revision cycles caused by a brief that was vague from the start. The strategic layer catches that before a single credit is spent on execution.

The work starts at a higher baseline. That's the compounding advantage.

02 / 03
Executional

33 agents. 9 models. Each job routed to the one that wins it.

Once the brief is sharp, the platform assembles the right agents and shows you the routing before you commit. Voice and tone to Sonnet. Conversion copy to GPT-5. Volume variants to Gemini. Campaign imagery to Flux.

One model behind everything is a margin decision, not a quality one. We built the stack differently — and we show you the routing so you can see exactly what you're getting.

The routing is the product. Remove any layer and the quality drops.

03 / 03
Human

Real people close the work that matters.

When craft matters — a pitch deck going to investors, a brand identity going to print, a campaign visual that has to own a room — the Caastor creative team finishes it. Same credit pool. Cost shown before you confirm.

Available from Tier 02 upward. 2–4 business day turnaround. One team that already knows your brand from the strategic layer up.

AI gets you 80% of the way, fast. Human craft closes the gap that actually shows.
