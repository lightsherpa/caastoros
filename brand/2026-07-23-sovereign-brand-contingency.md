**Re:** What happens if we can no longer use the Caastor brand
**From:** CMO desk
**Date:** 23 July 2026
**Companion to:** brand/2026-07-23-caastor-brand-architecture-review.md
**Nature:** Contingency architecture. Nothing here changes the public Route 2 posture. This is the sealed envelope.

**Legal caveat up front:** I am not a lawyer. Everything below that touches IP ownership depends on what the partnership agreement actually says. The first action in this memo is reading that agreement with counsel, not naming anything.

---

## 1. Reframe: you can lose the name, you must not lose the system

A forced rebrand kills companies that ARE their name. It barely dents companies whose name is a label on a system.

What the market is actually buying from us, per the validated pains and the shipped positioning: a marketing creative service SaaS where AI velocity is entwined with senior human criterio, built on a certified brand object, sold to founders and scale-ups who want AI creativity without junior results. Not one word of that sentence contains "Caastor."

So the contingency question is not "what do we rename to?" It is "which assets are portable, which are hostage, and what do we build now so that a split costs us a wordmark instead of a business?"

## 3. Firewall moves: do these now, in either future

These cost little, look innocent, and convert a catastrophic split into an inconvenient one. They are worth doing even if the partnership lasts forever.

1. **Name the methodology independently of the brand, and register it under your own entity.** Today the method is implicitly "the Caastor way." Give the certification workflow its own name (working label: the BIO Method, or a fresh coinage from section 5) and register the mark and domain under the entity you control alone. If the split comes, the market learns "the platform runs the X Method" and X is yours.
2. **Confirm and consolidate IP ownership in writing.** Codebase, BIO schema, Brandolph, agent specs, prompt systems. If ownership is joint or ambiguous, negotiate clarity now, while relations are good. Ambiguity always favors whoever is willing to litigate longer.
3. **Build equity in the unseizable asset: you.** Publish the thought leadership (brand intelligence, entwinement, criterio at scale) under Oscar Motta on oscarmotta.com and LinkedIn, with CaastorOS as the current vehicle, not the author. If the vehicle changes, the audience follows the author. This also serves the seed round either way.
4. **Own the relationship rails.** Newsletter list, CRM, community, demo pipeline: hosted on accounts your entity controls. An audience you can email is a brand you can rename.
5. **Hold one cleared alternative name in reserve.** One name, screened through EUIPO classes 35 and 42, domain and handles quietly acquired, a wordmark sketched, kept in a drawer. Cost: a few hundred euros and a weekend. Value in a split: three months of speed. Do not build more than one; a live alternative brand is a distraction and, if discovered, a provocation.
6. **Keep the human layer contractually close.** The senior certification layer is half the thesis. Make sure key-person agreements sit with your entity. [VERIFY WITH OSCAR]

## 4. Architecture options in the split scenario

### Option A: standalone new masterbrand, clean break

New name, new house, same system underneath. The three-altitude positioning ports verbatim; only Tier 2 and Tier 3 vocabulary (product names, plan names, character) gets rebuilt.

Best when: the split is public or messy, and distance from the partner is itself a message.
Cost: highest awareness restart, softened by firewall moves 3 and 4.

### Option B: endorsed launch under the personal masterbrand

The new platform launches as "by Oscar Motta" or "from the team behind 150+ brand transformations," leaning hard on the unseizable equity for 12-18 months, then the endorsement fades as the product brand stands up.

Best when: the cohort and pipeline trust you personally more than they trust any logo, which at private-preview stage is simply true.
Cost: couples the SaaS valuation story to a person for a while. Manageable if the endorsement is a launch ramp, not the architecture. Investors will push to see it fade; agree with them.

### Option C: methodology-led brand

The platform takes the name of the method (firewall move 1). The certification mark and the product share one identity: you sell the method, the software is how it ships.

Best when: firewall move 1 was done early and the method name accumulated 6+ months of public equity before the split.
Cost: method names can feel institutional. Needs a warm voice layer to avoid ISO-certification energy.

**Recommendation: B into A.** Launch endorsed, transition to standalone. And execute firewall move 1 immediately so Option C's asset exists whichever path we take.

## 5. Naming territories for the reserve name

Ground rules learned from the Caastor asset itself: native meaning in Spanish, pronounceable in English, short, metaphor that can grow an ecosystem of product vocabulary, and no dependence on the beaver. Everything below is a creative territory, not a cleared name; all candidates go through the full vetting protocol (trademark, domains, native-speaker, recall, collision) before entering the drawer.

### Territory 1: Criterio (judgment as the brand)

The thesis said out loud. Human judgment is the scarce ingredient; name the company after it.

| Candidate | Idea | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tino** | Spanish for good aim, sure judgment ("con tino") | Two syllables, warm, deeply idiomatic in the launch market, ownable-feeling | Meaning invisible to EN buyers until told; common personal name |
| **Pulso** | The steady hand ("a pulso": earned by skill) | Craft plus precision plus effort in one word; works as UI vocabulary (pulse states) | EN speakers read "pulse", acceptable drift |
| **Criterio** | The word itself | Instant thesis communication in ES | Generic word, weak registrability alone; likely needs a modifier |

### Territory 2: The seal (certification as the brand)

The BIO is certified by a senior director. The brand becomes the mark of that certification, the hallmark on AI-made work.

| Candidate | Idea | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sello** | Spanish for seal, stamp, hallmark | Perfectly literal to the certification moat; "con sello propio" is a gift of an idiom; short | EN pronunciation needs one exposure ("SEH-yo"); check ES trademark crowding |
| **Aval** | Spanish for backing, guarantee, endorsement | The human aval on AI output; four letters | Heavily used in LATAM banking (flag for Colombia ties); financial connotation |
| **Firma** | Signature, and also "the firm" | Double meaning is elegant: the human signature and the company | One letter from Figma in a design-adjacent category. Probably fatal; listed to show the territory's shape |

### Territory 3: The channel (flow directed by human structure)

Successor to the river heritage without the beaver: AI is the current, humans build the structure that makes it useful.

| Candidate | Idea | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cauce** | Spanish for riverbed, the channel that gives water direction | The entwinement thesis as landscape: raw flow, human-made course | EN pronunciation genuinely hard; likely ES-market ceiling |
| **Vega** | Fertile lowland beside a river | Keeps river heritage one step removed; short; astronomical secondary meaning adds lift; EN-friendly | Crowded surname and tech-name space; needs serious collision screening |
| **Norte** | Direction, purpose ("no perder el norte") | Strategy in one word; idiomatic and warm | Generic and likely crowded; directional metaphors age into cliché |

### Territory 4: Coined (built for ownership)

If every real word fails clearance, coin. Rules for the coinage: two or three syllables, ends in a vowel for ES comfort, no "AI" in the name (dates instantly), and it must be able to hold a character, an ecosystem vocabulary, and a certification mark. Generate 20, screen, keep one. Do not fall in love before the lawyer answers.

**My ranking: Territory 2 first, Territory 1 second.** The seal is the moat said as a name, and it is the hardest story for a generic-AI competitor to copy. Judgment is the thesis; certification is the proof. Brands built on proof travel further.

## 7. The 90-day break-free playbook (activate only on trigger)

**Days 1-15, quiet phase:** counsel reads the agreement; asset registry verified; reserve name pulled from the drawer and final-cleared; client contracts mapped by entity.
**Days 15-30, build phase:** wordmark and minimal visual system on the reserve name; website copy ported (the July 2026 canon is 90% find-and-replace because the positioning is name-independent); cohort communication drafted.
**Days 30-45, tell phase:** preview cohort told personally, by Oscar, before any public move. The message: same method, same humans, same system, new name; here is why you lose nothing. Risk-averse buyers forgive a rename told to their face; they never forgive discovering it on LinkedIn.
**Days 45-90, land phase:** public launch under Option B endorsement, redirect the owned rails (list, CRM, community) from firewall move 4, republish the method under its independent name, and never mention the old brand again in any asset.

---

## 8. Bottom line

The strongest brand architecture is the one where the name is the least valuable thing you own. Verify the IP registry this month, name and register the method under your own entity, build the public equity in Oscar Motta, keep one cleared name in a drawer, and then go back to selling CaastorOS at full speed with a clear conscience. Contingency done properly is invisible: the partner sees a healthy co-brand, and you sleep knowing the dam is not the only thing holding the water.
