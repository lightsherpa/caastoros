## PART 2 · MESSAGING HIERARCHY

**Level 0 · Category:** Brand intelligence operating system. Not a chatbot, not a prompt library, not a design subscription.

**Level 1 · Master message (the roof):**
Turn what your brand knows into work your team can trust.

**Level 1.5 · The entwinement thesis (the how, stated explicitly on the page):**
Not AI instead of people. Not people slowing down AI. Machine velocity and human judgment reinforcing each other: the Brand Steward certifies the truth, Brandolph directs, specialists produce, you approve. The creative output (strategy, copy, visuals, web) is the promise; the BIO is what makes it trustworthy, not the product itself.

**Level 2 · The transformation (the argument):**
FROM brand knowledge scattered across files and people, vague requests, disconnected AI outputs and repeated prompting. TO a reviewed source of truth, sharper briefs, purpose-built specialist crews, visible approvals and traceable on-brand work.

**Level 3 · Three pillars (the proof structure):**

1. **Consistency from a real source of truth.** Every specialist reads the same reviewed brand canon: positioning, audience, voice, visual rules and explicit refusals, scored and versioned. The Brand Intelligence Object (BIO).
2. **Senior judgment without prompt engineering.** You describe the business outcome. Brandolph, your AI brand director, sharpens the brief, names the strategic tension and assembles the smallest useful crew.
3. **Control before and after generation.** Crew and credits approved before anything runs. Every output inspectable, editable, flaggable and traceable to its brief, specialist and BIO version.

**Level 4 · Trust layer (the de-risking):**
A human Brand Steward certifies the BIO before it governs production. Human Craft is a separate, optional human-polish layer. Built on a real creative operation already serving brands in Europe and the Gulf, by a team with 20+ years and 150+ brands behind it.

**Level 4.5 · The compounding moat (customer-framed on the page, never the word "moat"):**
The stack that cannot be copied with a model upgrade: a certified BIO, plus Brandolph's persistent memory of every brief, approval and refusal, plus a senior human layer certifying and polishing. Generic AI starts from zero every task; the crew compounds. On-page framing: "accumulated judgment is infrastructure you own."

**Level 5 · Reason to act now:**
Private preview. Onboarding a limited cohort of brands during H2 2026. This scarcity is real, so it may be stated plainly.

**Sequencing rule:** business outcome first, mechanism second, technology last. The BIO is always explained in plain language before the acronym is used alone. Never lead with agent count; coordination and judgment are the story.

### SECTION 11 · FAQ

**Eyebrow:** FAQ

**Headline:** Fair questions. Straight answers.

**1. What is CaastorOS?**
CaastorOS is a brand intelligence operating system. It turns your scattered brand information, from your website to your guidelines and internal documents, into a reviewed source of truth, then coordinates an AI brand director and specialist agents that produce strategy, copy, visuals and web work governed by that truth. Less "another AI tool", more the system your brand runs on. (62 w)

**2. What is a Brand Intelligence Object?**
The BIO is your brand converted into a living, structured object: positioning, audience, voice, visual rules, goals, strategic constraints and explicit refusals, each connected to source evidence. It is scored for completeness and confidence, versioned so you can trace changes, and certified by a human Brand Steward before it governs any production. It is the single source of truth everything else reads. (63 w)

**3. What does Brandolph do?**
Brandolph is your AI brand director, not a chatbot. He reads your certified BIO before responding, turns an imprecise request into a decision-ready brief, asks focused questions when something matters, names the strategic tension behind the ask, and makes explicit recommendations and refusals. Then he proposes the smallest useful specialist crew and shows you the estimated credits before anything runs. (60 w)

**4. Do I need to know how to write AI prompts?**
No. That is rather the point. You describe the business change you need in plain language: "we are launching in Valencia", "our decks look junior". Brandolph does the translation into briefs and specialist instructions, informed by your BIO. If prompt expertise is the thing keeping your AI output usable, CaastorOS removes that dependency entirely. (55 w)

**5. What happens before credits are spent?**
Everything important. Brandolph sharpens the brief, asks his questions and proposes the crew with an estimated credit cost, all without spending anything. Credits only move after you approve the brief, the crew and the estimate. You will never discover the cost of a piece of work after it has been produced. (51 w)

**6. What can the specialists produce?**
Brand strategy and positioning, campaign concepts and naming, conversion, product, social, email and long-form copy, visual concepts, campaign imagery, social and advertising creative, landing pages, components, email and UX, plus competitive research, trend analysis, SEO and AEO work, insight synthesis and brand-consistency checks. Motion and sound are coming later and are not part of the MVP. (57 w)

**7. Why does the BIO need human review?**
Because AI extraction is fast but judgment is not optional. A Brand Steward, a human, reviews the compiled BIO, corrects what the machine misread, fills what the sources never said and certifies the result before it governs production. That review is why you can trust every downstream output. Confidence in the system starts with a human signature. (57 w)

**8. Can I edit or reuse the work?**
Yes. Every completed output lands in your Library, where you can inspect it, edit it, copy it, reuse it in new work, download it or remove it. Each piece keeps its lineage: the brief it answered, the specialist that produced it and the BIO version that governed it, so reuse never means losing context. (54 w)

**9. How does CaastorOS keep outputs on-brand?**
Three mechanisms working together. Every specialist reads the certified BIO before producing anything, so your voice, visual rules and refusals are loaded from the start. Brand QA specialists check consistency and voice quality against that same canon. And you approve, edit or flag every output on the canvas, with full traceability when something needs correcting. (55 w)

**10. Can I manage more than one brand?**
Yes. The Dam covers two brands and The River three, each with its own separate BIO and memory, so brands never bleed into each other. Studios and larger multi-brand teams run The Colony, with unlimited brands and centralized governance. Start with one brand and add the rest when the system has earned it. (53 w)

**11. What is Human Craft?**
Human Craft is an optional layer where senior human creatives polish eligible AI-produced work before it ships. It is deliberately separate from AI execution: machine work and human work are labelled as what they are, never blurred. The River includes priority Human Craft access; availability on other plans depends on your agreement. (52 w)

**12. How do MVP access and plans work?**
CaastorOS is in private preview, onboarding a limited cohort of brands during H2 2026. Plans scale by brand allowance, credits and Human Craft access: The Creek for one brand, The Dam for two, The River for three, The Colony for unlimited. Pilot access and commercial terms are agreed in conversation. Start by building your BIO or booking a demo. (60 w)

**13. How is our brand information handled?**
Your brand information is used to build and operate your BIO and to govern the work produced for you. Every claim inside the BIO stays linked to its source, and versioning keeps changes traceable. ### SECCIÓN 11 · FAQ

**1. ¿Qué es CaastorOS?**
CaastorOS es un sistema operativo de inteligencia de marca. **2. ¿Qué es un Brand Intelligence Object?**
El BIO es tu marca convertida en un objeto vivo y estructurado: posicionamiento, audiencia, voz, reglas visuales, objetivos, restricciones estratégicas y negativas explícitas, cada elemento conectado a su evidencia de origen. Brandolph es tu director de marca con IA, no un chatbot. Lee tu BIO certificado antes de responder, convierte una petición imprecisa en un brief listo para decidir, pregunta solo cuando algo importa, identifica la tensión estratégica detrás de la petición y recomienda con criterio, incluidas las negativas. No. Esa es justo la gracia. Describes el cambio de negocio que necesitas, en tu idioma: "lanzamos en Valencia", "nuestros decks parecen de junior". Brandolph hace la traducción a briefs e instrucciones para especialistas, apoyándose en tu BIO. Si la ingeniería de prompts es lo que mantiene usable tu producción con IA, CaastorOS elimina esa dependencia de raíz. (57 p)

**5. ¿Qué pasa antes de gastar créditos?**
Todo lo importante. Brandolph afina el brief, hace sus preguntas y propone el equipo con su coste estimado en créditos, sin gastar nada. Los créditos solo se mueven cuando apruebas el brief, el equipo y la estimación. Nunca descubrirás el coste de un trabajo después de que se haya producido. (49 p)

**6. ¿Qué pueden producir los especialistas?**
Estrategia de marca y posicionamiento, conceptos de campaña y naming, copy de conversión, producto, social, email y formato largo, conceptos visuales, imágenes de campaña, creatividades para social y publicidad, landing pages, componentes, email y UX, además de análisis competitivo, tendencias, SEO y AEO, síntesis de insights y controles de coherencia de marca. Porque la extracción con IA es rápida, pero el criterio no es opcional. Un Brand Steward, una persona, revisa el BIO compilado, corrige lo que la máquina malinterpretó, completa lo que las fuentes nunca dijeron y certifica el resultado antes de que gobierne la producción.
