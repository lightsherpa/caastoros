## Platform

web

## Users

The primary MVP buyer is a CMO or marketing lead at a growing company. They are responsible for keeping the brand coherent while a lean internal team, external partners, and AI tools produce increasing volumes of strategy, copy, visuals, and web work.

Their job is to turn scattered brand knowledge and business requests into approved, on-brand work without repeatedly rewriting prompts, briefing multiple specialists, or correcting the same brand mistakes.

## Product Purpose

CaastorOS turns a company’s website, guidelines, decks, and brand files into a reviewed source of truth, then uses that source to coordinate an AI brand director and specialist crews that produce strategy, copy, visuals, and web work.

Success means the marketing lead can move from an imprecise business need to a sharper brief, approve the proposed crew and credit estimate, and receive work that is inspectable and traceable to the brief and brand source that governed it.

## Positioning

CaastorOS is a brand intelligence operating system, not a chatbot, prompt library, or design subscription. Its meaningfully different mechanism combines:

- a structured, versioned Brand Intelligence Object (BIO);
- human certification of that brand truth by a Brand Steward;
- Brandolph, an AI brand director that reads the certified BIO and sharpens requests;
- purpose-built specialist crews assembled around the objective;
- approval of the brief, crew, and credits before production;
- outputs traceable to their brief, specialist, and BIO version.

The creative output is the promise. The BIO is the infrastructure that makes the output trustworthy.

## Operating Context

The buyer evaluates CaastorOS while dealing with brand knowledge spread across websites, guidelines, decks, drives, and people. Existing AI tools start from empty prompts and produce disconnected outputs with little persistent brand context.

The product workflow is:

1. Connect the website and upload brand files.
2. CaastorOS extracts and structures the brand canon into the BIO.
3. A human Brand Steward reviews and certifies the BIO.
4. The user describes the business change they need.
5. Brandolph sharpens the request and proposes the smallest useful specialist crew with estimated credits.
6. The user approves the brief, crew, and credits.
7. Work appears on an interactive canvas and can be approved, edited, or flagged.
8. Completed work remains available in the Library with its lineage.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The MVP produces strategy, copy, visuals, and web work.
- Specialist capabilities include strategy and positioning, campaign concepts and copy, social and lifecycle content, visual and web execution, research, and brand QA.
- Motion and sound are future capabilities and must not be presented as currently available.
- Human Craft is a separate optional layer in which senior humans polish eligible work.
- Credits are visible and approved before production runs.
- CaastorOS is in private preview and is onboarding a limited cohort during H2 2026.
- The primary website conversion is **Apply for the private pilot**.
- Pilot access and commercial terms are agreed in conversation.
- Pricing, unverified pilot counts, customer outcomes, testimonials, and conversion endpoints must not be invented.
- The public website must support English and Spanish.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: CaastorOS.
- The master message is: “Turn what your brand knows into work your team can trust.”
- Business outcome comes before mechanism; mechanism comes before technology.
- Human judgment and machine velocity are complementary, expressed through the “Entwined, not competing” thesis.
- The established Caastor palette uses bright yellow, near-black ink, white, and restrained neutral fields.
- The product vocabulary includes BIO, Brandolph, Brand Steward, specialist crews, Human Craft, credits, canvas, and Library. Plain-language explanations must precede acronyms and proprietary terminology.
- Voice is direct, intelligent, candid, and commercially grounded. It does not use inflated AI hype or fabricate proof.

## Product Principles

1. Demonstrate the mechanism instead of relying on AI claims.
2. Keep the buyer in control before and after production.
3. Explain proprietary concepts in plain language before naming them.
4. Treat human judgment as part of the system, not an apologetic add-on.
5. Make the private-preview path honest, singular, and easy to understand.
